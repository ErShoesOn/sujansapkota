## What is Bolpatra, and why do I need a bidder account?

Bolpatra is the public web address used for PPMO's electronic procurement system (e-GP) in Nepal. Contractors, suppliers and vendors use it to access tender notices, obtain documents and submit bids electronically. A registered bidder account is the starting point for taking part in most online public tenders.

If your firm is new to government tendering, registration is usually the first practical step after you have confirmed your firm's basic registrations and documents.

> **Important:** Portal screens, labels and checklists change over time. This guide explains the usual **shape** of the process. Always follow the current instructions, forms and help materials shown on the official portal.

## What information is usually needed before you begin?

Prepare the following information in one place before starting. The exact list depends on the current official checklist and the type of entity you are registering.

- Your firm's registered name, address and registration details.
- PAN/VAT and other tax-related information, where applicable.
- Details of the authorised person or representative who will use the account.
- A working mobile number and email address that your team can access.
- Scanned copies of registration, licence and authorisation documents.
- Bank information, if the current registration form asks for it.

**A practical tip:** use exactly the same firm name and spelling across all documents. Small inconsistencies are a common reason for queries later.

## How do I register a bidder account step by step?

1. **Open the official portal.** Go to [bolpatra.gov.np](https://bolpatra.gov.np/) and check that you are on the official PPMO e-GP site.
2. **Find the bidder registration option.** Look for the registration or new bidder link on the portal's current homepage.
3. **Enter firm details carefully.** Fill in the required fields using your official registration documents as the source of truth.
4. **Add representative details.** Enter the authorised person's information exactly as asked, including contact details you control.
5. **Upload the requested documents.** Use clear, readable scans in the formats and sizes the portal accepts.
6. **Submit the registration request.** Review every field before submitting, because corrections after submission may require a separate process.
7. **Complete verification and activation.** Follow the current instructions for account verification, confirmation or approval.
8. **Set up account security.** Configure the required security measures, such as two-factor authentication or digital signature requirements, before attempting a submission.

## What is Representative Authorization?

Representative Authorization is the formal way of telling the system who is allowed to act for your firm. In practice, it is about matching the portal's user roles with the people who actually prepare, review and submit your bids. It is worth getting right early: an unauthorised user can cause a submission to be rejected or a deadline to be missed.

Check the current portal guidance on roles, approvals and any documents needed for the person you authorise.

## What is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) in e-GP?

Two-Factor Authentication adds a second check after your password, usually a code sent to a registered device or generated by an app. It protects accounts that contain sensitive firm and bidding information. If your account uses 2FA or a digital signature, make sure the authorised user can access it before a tender deadline — not on submission day.

## Common problems and what to check

| Problem | What to check first |
|---|---|
| Registration cannot be submitted | Required fields, document formats and file sizes shown on the portal |
| Name or detail mismatch | Spelling and data across registration documents and the online form |
| Representative cannot access the account | Authorisation status, assigned role and registered contact details |
| Login or verification trouble | Registered mobile/email access, current security settings and helpdesk guidance |
| Digital signature not working | Browser/support requirements and current official instructions |

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a new account for every tender?

No. A bidder account is generally used across tenders, but each tender has its own document requirements, deadlines and submission steps. Always read the specific tender notice.

### Can someone register on my firm's behalf?

Registration should be done with your firm's authentic information and proper authorisation. An adviser can guide the process, but the firm's authorised representatives should control the account and its credentials.

### What should I do if the portal looks different from this guide?

Follow the current on-screen instructions and official PPMO materials. Portal interfaces change, and the official version always takes precedence over any third-party guide.

### Where can I check current official information?

Use the [PPMO website](https://ppmo.gov.np/) for rules and notices, and the [official e-GP portal](https://bolpatra.gov.np/) for registration and participation.

**Need help with your registration?** Call or message on WhatsApp and describe what you have completed so far.
