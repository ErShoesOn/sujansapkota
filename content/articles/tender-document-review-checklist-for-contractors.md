## Why should I review a tender document before preparing the bid?

A tender document is not just a set of forms. It defines who is eligible, what must be delivered, how a bid will be evaluated and what documents must be submitted. A careful first read helps you decide whether to bid, what to clarify and where mistakes are most likely.

This checklist is general guidance. The tender document, its amendments and the applicable official rules always take precedence.

## What should I check first?

Start with the basic facts:

- Procuring entity, tender title and identification number.
- Submission deadline, opening date and time.
- Bid validity period.
- The official portal where the bid must be submitted.
- Contact point for clarification and the last date to ask questions.
- Any amendments or corrigenda published after the original notice.

## How do I check eligibility honestly?

Read the eligibility requirements as written, not as you remember them from a previous tender.

1. Confirm the required contractor class, category or registration.
2. Check experience requirements and how they are measured.
3. Note financial criteria, if any.
4. Check whether joint ventures or consortia are allowed.
5. Look for exclusion or conflict-of-interest conditions.

If a requirement is unclear, ask for clarification through the stated channel before the deadline for questions.

## What should I look for in the technical section?

| Area | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Scope | Is the work, supply or service described clearly? |
| Specifications | Are drawings, specifications and standards complete? |
| Programme | Is there a required completion period or schedule? |
| Personnel | Are key staff qualifications required? |
| Site visit | Is a pre-bid meeting or site visit required? |
| Documents | Which technical documents must be uploaded? |

## What should I look for in the financial section?

- Confirm that the BOQ is the official version and that all amendments are applied.
- Check units, quantities and whether any page is missing.
- Note the bid security amount, format and validity requirement.
- Understand how taxes and deductions should be handled.
- Check currency, price validity and any price adjustment provisions.
- Confirm the payment terms and milestones.

## What should I verify before submission?

- All mandatory forms are completed with consistent information.
- Required signatures, dates and stamps are present.
- File names and formats follow the portal instructions.
- Corrigenda and amendments have been acknowledged where required.
- The technical and financial files are correctly placed in the portal.
- The submission acknowledgement is saved after uploading.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between eligibility and responsiveness?

Eligibility is about whether your firm meets the stated conditions to participate. Responsiveness is about whether your submitted bid meets the mandatory requirements of the tender document. A firm can be eligible and still submit a non-responsive bid.

### Should I bid if one document is missing?

It depends on whether that document is mandatory and whether it can be obtained in time. Treat mandatory requirements seriously and seek clarification where the rule is unclear.

### Can I rely on a checklist instead of the tender document?

No. A checklist is a reading aid. The tender document, its amendments and official instructions are the controlling sources.

### Can someone review my draft bid?

Yes. A focused document review can be requested as separate support. It is most useful when done well before the submission deadline.
