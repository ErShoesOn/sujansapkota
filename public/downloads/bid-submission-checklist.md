# Bid Submission Checklist

**General guidance for contractors participating in public tenders in Nepal.**

> This is a general working checklist, not a legal or official document. Requirements differ between tenders. Always follow the actual tender document, current PPMO instructions and the official e-GP portal.

## Before you start

- [ ] Confirm the tender notice and deadline on the official portal.
- [ ] Download the complete tender document and all amendments/corrigenda.
- [ ] Note the submission deadline, validity period and bid security requirement.
- [ ] Check your firm's eligibility against the stated criteria.

## Documents commonly checked

- [ ] Firm registration and trade licence documents.
- [ ] PAN/VAT and tax-related documents as requested.
- [ ] Documents showing relevant experience and completed works.
- [ ] Power of attorney / authorised representative documents.
- [ ] Bank details and bid security documents, where required.
- [ ] Any declarations or undertaking formats included in the tender document.

## Technical proposal

- [ ] All required forms completed with consistent information.
- [ ] Supporting documents attached in the requested order and format.
- [ ] Required signatures, dates and stamps included.
- [ ] Proposed work plan / methodology matches the tender scope.
- [ ] Key personnel and equipment details verified.

## Financial proposal

- [ ] BOQ downloaded from the official source and not altered incorrectly.
- [ ] Rates, quantities, units and totals checked.
- [ ] Taxes and deductions handled as stated in the tender document.
- [ ] Currency and price validity requirements followed.

## Online submission

- [ ] Bidder account and representative authorisation active.
- [ ] Required security settings (e.g. 2FA / digital signature) working.
- [ ] Files uploaded in the requested format and within size limits.
- [ ] Uploaded files opened once to confirm they are readable.
- [ ] Submission completed before the deadline, with acknowledgement saved.

## Final reminder

Keep a complete digital copy of the submitted bid and all confirmations for your records.
