# Tender Document Review Checklist

**A structured first read of a public tender document in Nepal.**

> This is a general working tool, not legal advice. The tender document, its amendments and the applicable official rules always take precedence.

## 1. Basic tender information

- [ ] Procuring entity and tender title.
- [ ] Tender identification number.
- [ ] Submission deadline and opening date/time.
- [ ] Bid validity period.
- [ ] Contact point for clarification.

## 2. Eligibility

- [ ] Contractor class/category requirement.
- [ ] Registration and licence requirements.
- [ ] Experience requirements and how they are measured.
- [ ] Financial criteria, if any.
- [ ] Joint venture or consortium rules, if applicable.
- [ ] Exclusion or conflict-of-interest conditions.

## 3. Scope and technical requirements

- [ ] Works, goods or services described clearly.
- [ ] Drawings, specifications and BOQ available and complete.
- [ ] Required work plan, methodology or personnel.
- [ ] Site visit or pre-bid meeting requirement.
- [ ] Key deliverables and completion period.

## 4. Financial requirements

- [ ] BOQ format and instructions.
- [ ] Bid security amount, format and validity.
- [ ] Taxes, duties and price adjustment conditions.
- [ ] Payment terms and milestones.
- [ ] Currency and price validity rules.

## 5. Submission and compliance

- [ ] Online submission requirement and portal.
- [ ] File formats, naming and size limits.
- [ ] Mandatory forms and signature requirements.
- [ ] Documents to upload with the technical and financial bid.
- [ ] Amendments/corrigenda checked and acknowledged.

## 6. Evaluation

- [ ] Evaluation method stated in the document.
- [ ] Pass/fail criteria clearly identified.
- [ ] Weighting or scoring system, if any.
- [ ] Documents that must be submitted to prove compliance.

## Decision

After the review, record one of three outcomes: **proceed**, **proceed with conditions**, or **do not bid**. Note the reasons so the decision can be revisited later.
